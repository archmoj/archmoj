* **Interdisciplinary Foundation:** I bring together a background in architectural design and planning with software development in weather/climate and 3D graphics.
* **Decades of Evolution:** Over the past twenty years, I founded **SOLARCHVISION** and grew it from an award-winning Master's thesis into a comprehensive BIM-6D platform.
* **Scenario & Impact Modeling:** I develop practical ways to turn climate and forecast ensembles into clear design scenarios and new metrics.
* **Data-to-3D Pipeline:** I work across the full stack - from binary weather formats to building interactive 3D visuals on the desktop and in the browser.
* **Public & Open-Source Impact:** My software work has contributed to projects with Canadian weather forecasting and building energy agencies, advanced various 3D CAD tools, and open-source graphing libraries like **plotly.js**.
